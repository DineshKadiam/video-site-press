This was created to study erratic behavior in OKLCH at high chroma values. For instance between 40-100% chroma, an OKLCH color with 0-20% lightness and a hue somewhere in the nominal red-yellow spectrum (e.g. at 85deg hue) starts to produce saturated mid-tone greens, which is erratic both in terms of hue and lightness.

Usage: Move the slider to raise/lower chroma and see what outputs appear across the spectrum.
